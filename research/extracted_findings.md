# Key findings extracted from `ai_battle` (short version)

1. **Rice is the right first crop.** It has the strongest Bangladesh-origin image data:
   - 9,769 field photos in RiceLeafDiseaseBD v3 [WEB ✓]
   - three more field sets from other districts [WEB ✓]
2. **Image diagnosis already exists in Bangladesh** (Rice Solution, Dr. Chashi, BAMIS, Plantix). Compete on trust and accessibility, not on "AI exists".
3. **Most defensible innovation:** a calibrated "not sure" answer + Bangla audio + published accuracy on unseen districts.
4. **Safety line agreed across the team files:**
   - no AI-written pesticide doses
   - refer to an officer or **16123**
   - preliminary screening, not diagnosis
5. **Low literacy and weak networks** are recurring themes. Rural internet use is 43.6% vs 75.7% urban (BBS ICT, reported by the [Daily Star](https://online-d11.thedailystar.net/business/economy/news/rural-areas-lag-behind-digital-divide-persists-4153326)). This supports audio and lightweight in-browser inference.
6. **Claims to never make:**
   - "first or only"
   - an accuracy number before training
   - "replaces the officer"
   - a partnership with DAE or BRRI
7. **Scope creep in the team idea files** (price prediction, marketplace, digital twin, IVR, insect ID, yield). These are future ideas only, not for this build.
